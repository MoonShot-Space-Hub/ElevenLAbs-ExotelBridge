## What can be made better

### High priority (fixed in the edit)
1. **"Free" arrives in the last 2 seconds.** The offer (a *free* masterclass) is only said at 46.6 s, and most viewers are gone by then. *Edit:* "THIS DIWALI • FREE MASTERCLASS" is on screen from frame 0, and the end card repeats it.
2. **Nothing works with the sound off.** There was no text at all. *Edit:* captions throughout (keywords in amber), plus graphics that carry the key lists.
3. **48 s of one static medium shot.** The only cut is a tiny jump at 33.7 s. *Edit:* virtual camera cuts (wide / medium / close) every 2–4 s on phrase boundaries, and the jump cut becomes one of them.
4. **Seven learning outcomes are rattled off in 7 s.** *Edit:* a "What you'll learn" grid that fills in word by word.
5. **The product name is said once and never shown.** *Edit:* a "1 simple masterclass → ABCT with AI" reveal as the big moment, and the name again on the end card.
6. **"Click on the link" points at nothing and the video just stops.** *Edit:* an end card with "Join the free masterclass", a JOIN NOW button, "Tap the button below 👇", arrows toward the ad's CTA button, and a 2 s hold.
7. **Compliance (SEBI / ASCI).** "Parallel source of income" is said three times, along with "anybody can trade with AI" and "automate the stock". In a trading-education ad these read as income or ease-of-profit promises. *Edit:* no money imagery anywhere, "income" is never highlighted, a persistent disclaimer strip, and a full legal line on the end card ("…No assured returns or income").

### Medium
8. **Background:** the magenta mandala set is on-theme for Diwali, but the bright gold mandalas behind the head compete with the face. *Edit:* light blur and darkening via person-matting (more under cards).
9. **Audio:** a clean studio voice, slightly boxy, with no music or SFX. *Edit:* light EQ and air, a synthesized music bed arranged to the script, subtle SFX, and loudness at −14 LUFS.
10. **Framing:** the black studio floor edge shows at the very bottom of the frame. *Edit:* cropped out by the base punch-in.
