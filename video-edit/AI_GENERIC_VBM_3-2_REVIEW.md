## What can be made better

### High priority (fixed in the edit)
1. **The offer only arrives at 43 s.** The hook ("28 hours a day") is good, but the masterclass is first mentioned 43 seconds in. *Edit:* an "AI MASTERCLASS" pill sits on the hook card from frame 0, and the end card repeats it.
2. **Nothing works with the sound off.** *Edit:* captions throughout with amber keywords, and graphics that carry each list.
3. **49 s of one static medium shot.** *Edit:* virtual camera changes (wide, medium and close) on phrase boundaries. The single jump cut at 40.3 s becomes one of them.
4. **The lists go by unseen:**
   - "no time for health / family / fitness / hobbies" becomes a ✗ grid.
   - "job / business / trading & investments / personal life / travel plans / everywhere" becomes a use-case grid that fills in word by word.
   - "health / kids / family" becomes a ✓ grid that mirrors the ✗ one.
5. **The key promise isn't emphasised.** That line is "buy back at least 10 hours of your week". *Edit:* a counter card that ticks up to 10 hours.
6. **The credibility line goes by fast.** That line is "not a tech guy, a stock market investor, uses AI daily". *Edit:* an "About me" card with "Tech guy" struck through, then a "My routine" card.
7. **The audio is very quiet** (about −29.5 LUFS, roughly 15 dB under the feed) and has low rumble. *Edit:* rumble filtered, EQ, compression, mastered to −14 LUFS, with a music bed and subtle SFX.
8. **"Click on the link" points at nothing.** *Edit:* an end card ("Learn AI the simple way", "Join my masterclass", JOIN NOW, "Tap the button below 👇", arrows) and a 2 s hold.

### Medium
9. **The white table fills the bottom ~30% of the frame.** Captions placed in the usual spot would sit on bright white and be hard to read. *Edit:* captions moved up to the jacket line, and the background (shelf lights) slightly darkened behind the cards.
10. **Claims:** "buy back 10 hours a week" and "stock market investor… trading and investments" are time and finance claims. *Edit:* shown only as spoken, with no money imagery, plus a light "Educational content only • Not investment advice" line and an end legal line.
